# Recorded dice roll

`dice-roll.mp3` is the recording used by the local edition, retained here as an
MP3 conversion of **rolling dice 2.wav**, by **nettimato** (2016-09-11).

- Original work and CC0 dedication: https://freesound.org/people/nettimato/sounds/353974/
- MP3 distribution source: https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-rolling-dice-2-102706/
- Original Freesound filename: `rolling dice 2.wav`.
- CC0 legal text: [LICENSE-CC0.md](LICENSE-CC0.md).

The build embeds the MP3 bytes for offline playback. Other effects, ambience,
and music are generated by the project's own sound engine.

SHA-256: `dcb77fbb6c40563249db034c61a0330113575541dd65d04b54d4f139da3dd739`.
