# Content and integration notices

Lantern Harbor's locations and stories are fictional. Its board graphics,
property-record layout, twenty harbor badges, lantern icon, and event prose
were created for this edition. Original source and assets use the MIT license.
The project uses system fonts and includes no commercial board artwork.

## Audio

The bundled dice roll is an MP3 conversion of **rolling dice 2.wav** by
**nettimato**, released under CC0 on
[Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/nettimato/sounds/353974/).
See [audio provenance and license](assets/audio/README.md). Other effects,
clubhouse ambience, and the original composition **After Hours at the Table**
are synthesized locally by the project.
